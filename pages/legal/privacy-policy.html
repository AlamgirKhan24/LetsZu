Privacy policy

How this demo store handles information.

What stays in your browser

Your cart and wishlist are saved in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload. They never leave your device, and you can clear them any time by clearing site data in your browser.

What we do not collect

This demo has no server. Contact, sign-up, log-in, newsletter and checkout forms are checked in your browser but their contents are not sent, stored or shared. No payment details are requested and no orders are placed.

Third-party services

To display the site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, Bootstrap from jsDelivr and photographs from Unsplash. Those providers receive your IP address and browser details when they serve those files, under their own privacy policies.
