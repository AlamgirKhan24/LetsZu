Our story

We started LetsZu to bring honest, freshly packed tea to everyday cups.

From garden to kitchen

We buy directly from small gardens in Japan, China, India and Kashmir, taste every lot ourselves, and pack in small batches so the leaf arrives as fresh as possible.

Chai from our home

Our masala chai blend is built around the spice mix we grew up with in Peshawar: cardamom, cinnamon, clove and ginger over strong black tea.

How tea is made

1. Pluck

Young shoots are picked by hand.

2. Wither and roll

Leaves soften and release their flavour.

3. Oxidise or steam

This step decides black, green or oolong.

4. Dry and pack

Sealed in tins within days.
